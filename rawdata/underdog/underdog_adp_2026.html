Biggest Fantasy Football ADP Movers

Mike Washington Jr, RB, Raiders — Rising

Given the injury to Ashton Jeanty, it is not surprising to see Mike Washington Jr. quickly climbing in ADP.

It now looks like Jeanty could be ready as early as Week 1, which means that rise was probably an overreaction, but ESPN's Ryan McFadden highlighted an interesting quote from head coach Klint Kubiak.

Here is #Raiders HC Klint Kubiak full response on using Ashton Jeanty and Mike Washington Jr. as a 1-2 punch in the backfield.

In May, Kubiak talked about using Jeanty as a bell cow running back. It looks like Washington has changed the staff’s perspective of the RB room. pic.twitter.com/IhMWeZuFuE

— Ryan McFadden (@ryanmcfadden_) August 28, 2026

Washington has multiple outs.

Perhaps Jeanty's recovery takes longer than it appears it will based on current reports.

Perhaps Jeanty suffers another injury in the future, especially if he rushes back from this issue.

Perhaps Washington gets enough carries and is explosive enough, as we have seen in the preseason, to make those count.

The most likely outcome, though, is that he is a 5 to 10 touch player on an offense that could struggle against a tough schedule.

That means he is probably overpriced at his new higher ADP.

Oronde Gadsden II, TE, Chargers — Falling

One of our earliest calls of the offseason was to be completely out on Oronde Gadsden II, and the preseason has made that look like the right decision.

Gadsden has consistently worked behind both Charlie Kolar and David Njoku, new additions who seemingly fit better as the combo receiving-blocking options required in bigger sets.

Gadsden had just a 65.6% route rate last season in the games in which he played, which would have ranked 17th among qualified tight ends.

Now it looks like that route rate will be cut even further.

Gadsden was effective when targeted as a rookie.

He averaged 9.6 yards per target and 13.6 yards per reception.

Those numbers were fifth and sixth among 55 tight ends who ran at least 150 routes last year.

He should produce if he finds his way onto the field, but this preseason has raised serious questions about how much he will actually be on the field to start the season.

At this point, Gadsden looks undraftable in standard-sized redraft leagues.
