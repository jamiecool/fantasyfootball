The board

PAR = projected points above the last starter at the position. PAR/$ is value per dollar — the column to sort on. Click any header to sort.

Did each price tier return its share of the value?

Share of value delivered ÷ share of money spent, over 1,514 skill picks across nine seasons. Above 1.0 means the tier returned more than it cost. This uses actual outcomes — no projections.

Chance a pick returned a startable season

Startable = finished inside the league's starter count at the position (top 12 QB / 24 RB / 36 WR / 12 TE). Cells with fewer than 25 picks are marked — they cannot support a conclusion.

Share of league spend by position

Percent of the $2,400 pool spent at each position, by season.

FFC vs Underdog ADP

Each dot is a player. The diagonal is agreement. Dots above it are drafted earlier on Underdog than on FFC; below, the reverse. The board uses FFC, so a big gap means the price could be materially wrong for this league.

Biggest disagreements

Sorted by absolute gap. These are the players whose price is least trustworthy.

Every pick

Exp is the median finish this league has historically got from that price band, over nine seasons. vs exp is the gap in positional ranks — positive means the pick beat its price. Shading is by production gained, not rank: a rank gap means far more at the top of a position (RB1→RB6 is 80 points; RB50→RB55 is 6), so the colour uses points measured in median-starter units. Hover any cell for the value. Rows marked est had their price reconstructed (drafted then dropped, so Yahoo no longer stored what was paid).

By team

What each franchise spent and what the drafted players returned. Points-for is the team's actual season total; draft points are what these 16 picks scored, which ignores in-season moves.
