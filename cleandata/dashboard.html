The board

rank is the positional rank, weighted 75% market ADP / 25% projection — the board's job is what the room will pay, and two independent markets agree with each other far more (ρ 0.95) than either agrees with any single projection model (0.65–0.72), so ADP carries it. mkt and proj show the two inputs. trend is Underdog ADP movement since its last update — positive means the market is moving toward him. Did each price tier return its share of the value?

Share of value delivered ÷ share of money spent, over 1,514 skill picks across nine seasons. Above 1.0 means the tier returned more than it cost. This uses actual outcomes — no projections.
