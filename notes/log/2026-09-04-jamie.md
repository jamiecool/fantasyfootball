## The method — do not regress these

1. **Projection for a positional slot = what the Nth player TAKEN at that position scored**,
   averaged 2023–25, then a least-squares fit `pts = a + b·ln(rank) + c·rank` per position
   (checked monotone, isotonic fallback). Nth-**taken**, not Nth-**finisher**: finisher
   averages are order statistics, and an earlier attempt on them put DEF1 at pick 3.07.
2. **Baseline = Bryant's draft-demand rule** — count of each position the room takes in the
   first 100 picks, averaged → **QB23 / RB28 / WR40 / TE8**. Not worst-starter, not
   waiver-wire. K and DEF are listed as fill, not valued (the room takes ~0 in the top 100).
3. **Ordering within position = Underdog 2026 ADP** (`rawdata/underdog/underdog_adp_2026.csv`,
   refreshed Sep 3). K/DEF ordering from the ESPN board.
4. **Value = slot projection − baseline projection.** The board ranks on value.

Output: first three rounds are 6 QB / 13 RB / 17 WR / 0 TE, stable across baseline horizons
80/100/120. Fit R² by position — how much of scoring the room's draft order explains:
RB .38, WR .36, QB .13, TE .09.

## Findings accepted (method-independent checks)

- **Round bands.** The room's actual picks 2023–25, value over baseline: rounds 1–2 QB +63 vs
  RB +119 / WR +116; rounds 3–4 QB +26 vs RB +82 / WR +59; rounds 7–9 QB +23. QB5–12 taken
  averaged 218, QB13–24 averaged 221 — indistinguishable. Only QB1–4 (302 avg) separate.
- **Structural.** The lineup is QB / 2RB / 3WR / TE / SUPERFLEX / FLEX = **8 non-QB skill
  starters against 6** in a standard superflex league, so published SF rankings are
  structurally too QB-heavy for this room.
- **RB vs WR.** Equal at the top; RB cliffs around RB15, WR holds value deep (WR24 +32 vs
  RB24 +13). FLEX leans WR; RB is early-or-never; TE is Bowers/McBride or stream.
- **2022 out-of-sample** (same format; 2021 was 14 teams / 2 WR and is not comparable):
  QB1–4 +119 vs RB1–6 +98 / WR1–6 +105; QB5–12 +46 vs RB7–12 +58 / WR7–12 +76. Mid-QB ranges
  from par (2022) to zero (2023–25). **The board currently reflects the pessimistic end.**

## Data facts worth not rediscovering

- `rawdata/ppp/draft{yr}.psv` schema:
  `overall|round|pick|teamId|playerId|keeper|name|pos|actual|proj|gp`. `proj` is 0 for most
  2023 picks — ESPN did not retain it. `board2026.psv`:
  `pid|name|pos|proTeam|espnRank|adp|own|proj`.
- `league.json` carries scoring (full PPR, 4 pass TD, −2 INT, distance FGs, D/ST PA and YA
  ladders), starters, and `teamsBySeason`. 2021 = 14 teams / 2 WR.
- **ADP sign conventions differ by source.** Sharp: `adp_delta = prev − current` (+ = rising).
  4for4: `ADP Change = current − prev` (− = rising). Normalise before merging. (Repo
  convention is Sharp's — trap 7 in `CLAUDE.md`.)
- **Underdog has no public API.** Use the Sharp Football table (`fetch_underdog_adp.py`) or
  the 4for4 Shiny app (`apps.4for4.com/UD_ADP/`, set `date_range:shiny.date`, single JS
  execution) for windows.
- ESPN endpoints: `/seasons/{yr}/segments/0/leagues/{id}` is public for 2025–26;
  `/leagueHistory/{id}?seasonId=` needs espn_s2 + SWID and returns an **array**;
  `kona_player_info` batches ≤ 40 ids.
- ~~Hub publishing is disabled org-wide.~~ **Wrong as of 2026-09-04** — `get_hub_knowledge` returns `enabledFeatures.publishing: true` (docs version 2026-09-03.1). The dashboard is published at https://hub.p3adaptive.com/reports/pbaffl-draft-data, now v8.

## Later the same day — the value board, questioned and then wired in

An interactive version of `ppp_vor_board.csv` was published as a Claude artifact
(https://claude.ai/code/artifact/48785f90-902c-4156-b545-246ad2bf7c94). Looking at it,
Jamie was surprised WR came out so high (17 of the first 36). Three checks followed.

**1. The WR-heavy mix is not robust, and the culprit is the fitted baseline, not the
baseline rank.** Strict last-starter ranks (QB24/RB24/WR36/TE12) still give 16 WR / 12 RB.
But the fitted curve reads WR40 at 150 pts where the raw data around the slot says 165–188,
and RB28 at 154 where the data says 128–145. A baseline shifts an entire position uniformly,
so that is a ~45-point WR-vs-RB swing on every player. Any shape-free baseline flips it:

| baseline at the same ranks | QB | RB | WR | TE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fitted curve (in use) | 6 | 13 | 17 | 0 |
| raw window ±3 | 4 | 20 | 11 | 1 |
| isotonic | 4 | 19 | 12 | 1 |

Windows ±1…±8 all land at 16–22 RB / 11–15 WR. The caveat cuts both ways: baseline slots
are very noisy (RB28 scored 214, 205, 13 across the three seasons; se ≈ 30–53 pts), so the
data cannot firmly decide either. Adding 2022 halves the standard errors and narrows the
gap to 16 WR / 13 RB, direction unchanged. Backlog item 10.

**2. Why published superflex rankings are more QB-heavy than anything here.** Draft Sharks
puts 6 QBs in round 1 and QB12 at overall #25. Recomputing this board with a *common*
flex-level replacement (152 pts) for everyone who can fill the superflex reproduces that
almost exactly (5 QB per 12-pick band, QB12 at #29): a QB at "zero value" on this board
(QB23 = 199) outscores a flex-level WR by +47, and published rankings credit every startable
QB with that premium.

**3. …and why that convention is wrong for this room (Jamie's call, and the data agrees).**
Every team drafted ≥2 QBs in 2023, 2024 and 2025 (min 2, median 3; 33–35 QBs drafted;
QB25+ still scored 150–360). Nobody has ever been shut out, so the superflex is a QB slot
for all twelve teams; the +47 is collected by everyone and confers no relative advantage.
The replacement for skipping QB12 is a later QB, not a WR — the position-specific baseline
is correct here. The common baseline is the right hedge only for rooms where QBs run out,
which generic rankings must assume. Two accepted findings therefore stand: early QBs
returned +64 vs RB +107 / WR +118 in rounds 1–2, and the 8-vs-6 structural point.

**Built:** the value board is now a pivot on the dashboard's PPP board (pick curve |
value over baseline). `build_ppp_vor_board.py` is a `build_all.py` stage before
`build_ppp_data.py`, which joins `vb*` fields onto each board row (177 of 184 valued players

re-ranks densely inside the pool so the Move column stays like-for-like. Pivot is a

orders the planner pool.

## Third pivot — Yahoo's analysts ordering within position

Jamie asked for a pivot that uses Yahoo's full-PPR rankings article for the intra-positional
order. **The article carries no list in its markup**: the table is a FantasyPros partner
widget (`partners.fantasypros.com/external/widget/fp-widget.php`) filtered to Yahoo's six
analysts by FantasyPros expert ID (`filters=9:317:747:1408:7604:7666`), and the widget's JS
calls `partners.fantasypros.com/api/v1/consensus-rankings.php` with the same parameters. So
`fetch_yahoo_rankings.py` pulls that JSON — it *is* the article, not a FantasyPros consensus.
379 players, 6 experts, "Draft PPR", updated 9/04; ECR 1..379 complete; Gibbs / Chase / Nacua
on top, matching the page. Sanity checks in the fetcher: year, expert count, completeness,
per-position floors (trap 3).

Wired as pivot three on the PPP board: **Yahoo orders within a position, the pick curve stays
across** — the mirror image of the value pivot, which kept Underdog and swapped the
cross-position half. Built identically to `build_board()`'s Underdog step (Yahoo-ranked first
in ECR order, then anyone Yahoo lacks in ESPN ADP order), joined as `yh*` fields, dense rank
inside the ESPN pool. Yahoo ranks K and D/ST too, so those follow Yahoo on this pivot
(team-code aliases JAC→JAX, WAS→WSH, LA→LAR for the `def_<team>` keys). Every one of the 249
ESPN-pool players has a Yahoo rank; the only Yahoo names missing from ESPN's pool are deep
rookies (Emmett Johnson, Kaelon Black, Pat Bryant, Cyrus Allen, Chris Bell, Malik Davis).

Where the two markets disagree most inside the top 60: Mahomes QB11 → QB17 (#29 → #60 on the
board), A.J. Brown WR7 → WR11, Rashee Rice WR13 → WR18, Malik Nabers WR9 → WR13; the other
way, Achane RB12 → RB9, Olave WR12 → WR9, Waddle WR19 → WR15, Kyler QB17 → QB15. Yahoo's
overall order is a one-QB list (Allen #31) and is never used.

`fetch_yahoo_rankings.py` is a live-feed stage in `build_all.py` (runs with `--refresh`);

## 2026-09-06 — draft-night budget tracking, and D/ST on the NFL stats tab

**Paid prices in the auction planner.** Jamie's draft-night spreadsheet did three things:
favourites with prices, his roster as it formed, and how far off plan he was running. The
planner already did the first two; it now has a `paid` box per roster slot. One price typed
in flips the meter to real money — paid / $200, $ left per open spot (red under the $2
floor), *vs board* (paid minus board price, the "off plan" number), bought vs still-planned
— and the Compare table gains the same rows. Plans store it as `paid: {name: $}`;
`serve.py` needed no change. Entering only his own picks is the design; nobody has time to
log the room's other 190.

**D/ST game logs.** "Def stats are missing from the NFL stats section" — they were, because a
defence has no row in nflverse's player file. New `fetch_nflverse_team.py` pulls
`stats_team_week_{2017..2025}.csv` and `games.csv` (both gitignored mirrors, skip-if-present,
a `build_all.py` stage keyed on the 2025 file). `build_clean_data.py` scores one row per
team-week under PBAFFL's D/ST rules — sack 1, INT 2, FR 2, TD 6, safety 2, block 2,
2-pt return 2, points-allowed ladder 10/7/4/1/0/−1/−4 — and appends them to `player_weeks`
as position `DEF`, key `def_<code>` (LA→LAR so both boards' defence keys resolve). Yards
allowed comes from the opponent's team-week row (net yards); points allowed from
`games.csv`, with `NFL_MOVES` applied because games.csv keeps OAK for 2017–19 while the
team file says LV — the first pass silently lost the Raiders for three seasons (31 teams).
BUF/CIN 2022 at 16 games is the cancelled game, not a bug.

The page re-scores D/ST for Perennial Push from the stat columns (ESPN's PA ladder
5/4/3/1/0/−1/−3/−5 plus a yards-allowed ladder), recognising a D/ST game row by its length:
the nine D/ST columns ride at the end of the row and only on DEF rows, so 55k player rows
do not each carry nine zeros. `nAgg` reads a missing cell as 0. New `NCOLS.DEF` column set
(Sck INT FR TD Saf Blk PtsA YdsA), `D/ST` in the position filter. Spot check: NE 2019 week 2
